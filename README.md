# 🌱 EcoNano AI
### AI-Powered Sustainable Nanomaterial Selection for Water Remediation

## Overview
EcoNano AI is an interactive machine-learning prototype designed to explore how nanomaterials could support sustainable water treatment.

The platform compares potential nanomaterials for different pollutant categories and explores how environmental conditions, such as pH, pollutant concentration, and contact time, may influence predicted removal efficiency.

## 🌍 Problem
Water pollution caused by dyes, pharmaceutical residues, and toxic metal ions is a major environmental challenge. Conventional treatment methods can be costly and may generate additional waste.

Nanomaterials offer promising opportunities for pollutant removal, but selecting suitable materials and treatment conditions requires careful investigation.

## 💡 Our Solution
EcoNano AI combines machine learning and environmental analysis in an interactive dashboard.

Users can:
- Explore different nanomaterial candidates.
- Select pollutant categories.
- Adjust pH, pollutant concentration, and contact time.
- Compare predicted removal efficiencies.
- Explore model feature importance.
- Review environmental sustainability considerations.

## 🧪 Materials Explored

- Titanium dioxide (TiO₂)
- Magnetite (Fe₃O₄)
- Biochar-based composites

## ⚠️ Scientific Disclaimer
This prototype uses a synthetic demonstration dataset. Its predictions are illustrative and are not experimentally validated measurements.

The application does not establish real-world treatment effectiveness, nanoparticle safety, or environmental suitability. Experimental validation and toxicity assessment would be necessary before practical application.

## 🌿 Expected Environmental Impact
The project aims to support research and awareness around:
- Sustainable water remediation.
- Responsible nanomaterial selection.
- Data-driven environmental research.
- Reduced uncertainty in early-stage treatment exploration.
